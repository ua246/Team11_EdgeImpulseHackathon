Flicker test page

Warning: this page flashes at seizure-provoking rates. Don't use it near anyone with photosensitive epilepsy, and don't look at it yourself. Point the sensor at the screen and shield the rest of the room.

Click a rate to start (full screen). Press Esc or click to stop.

The screen refreshes about 60 times a second, so it can only show square waves up to 30 Hz; rates that don't divide 60 evenly have slightly uneven flashes, but the average rate is exact. Use with the light test and sensor-stream (live readout) and for Edge Impulse data collection (tools/unoq record flicker_18hz 30 while it runs).
