Flicker test page

Warning: this page flashes at seizure-provoking rates. Don't use it near anyone with photosensitive epilepsy, and don't look at it yourself. Point the sensor at the screen and shield the rest of the room.

Click a rate to start (full screen). Press Esc or click to stop.

Automatic sweep for apps/light-test (about 2 min): black, a 3 s sync block, then 1–30 Hz for 6 s each with black gaps. Hold the sensor 2–5 cm from the middle of the screen, facing it, with the screen at full brightness. Analyse afterwards with screen_sweep.py.

Training data for the Edge Impulse model (apps/flashguard, about 7 min): a sync block, then 48 labelled 7 s segments (flicker at 3–30 Hz in several colours and contrasts; steady light, slow blinks, fades and level steps), then 60 s of room light. Prop the sensor 2–5 cm from the middle of the screen; when the screen says so, point it around the room instead. Then run tools/ei/make_dataset.py.

The screen refreshes about 60 times a second, so it can only show square waves up to 30 Hz; rates that don't divide 60 evenly have slightly uneven flashes, but the average rate is exact.
